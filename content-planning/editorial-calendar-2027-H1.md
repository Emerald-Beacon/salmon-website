## Strategic Summary

The library is now **large and dense (~156 posts)**. The 2026-H2 plan filled the biggest
gaps (opened the Mini-Split cluster, deepened Heat Pump and Commercial/VRF, and refreshed
the entire early-2025 decay batch plus the annual winter seasonals). The win for H1 2027 is
**not more AC/Furnace/Maintenance content** — those clusters are saturated and at
cannibalization risk. The wins are:

1. **Finish the Mini-Split / Ductless cluster** — it grew from 2 → ~4 posts in H2. Two more
   spokes (single- vs. multi-zone, ductless maintenance) cross it past ~75% coverage.
2. **Own the post-25C rebate story** — the federal **25C Energy Efficient Home Improvement
   Credit terminated for equipment placed in service after Dec 31, 2025** (One Big Beautiful
   Bill Act, PL 119-21). Buyers who expect a federal credit in 2027 will be surprised. There
   is a real, high-intent content gap: *what's actually left* (Utah utility rebates — Dominion
   Energy ThermWise, Rocky Mountain Power wattsmart — plus any state programs).
   **Do NOT write a 2027 post that promises a federal 25C credit for new installs.**
3. **Refresh the 24-month-old early-2025 batch** — 30+ posts published Jan–Mar 2025 are now
   ~24 months old. These are the H1 refresh pool; AI citations favor content updated recently.
4. **Ride the season** — deep winter + inversion (Jan–Feb) → spring AC tune-up pivot (Mar–Apr)
   → summer cooling ramp + install decisions (May–Jun).
